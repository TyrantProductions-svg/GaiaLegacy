## Physical atlas options

| Option | Pages | Sampling | Dimensions | RGBA8 bytes | Estimated bind/draw runs |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Composite | 1 | LINEAR | 256x512 | 524,288 | 1 |
| Split | 2 | Pixelify NEAREST; Inter LINEAR | 256x512 + 256x256 | 786,432 | 4 |

Both options render 253 non-space glyph quads for the closed specimen and stay
well below the 2 MiB review target. The split option costs 262,144 additional
bytes and three additional page runs in the deliberately alternating specimen,
but preserves independent sampling for Pixelify and Inter without adding a
second renderer.

## Recommendation pending approval

- Production families: Pixelify Sans 700/600 for display roles and Inter
  400/500/600 for functional UI, HUD, and body.
- Physical pages: two deterministic pages, Pixelify with NEAREST sampling and
  Inter with LINEAR sampling.
- IBM Plex Sans remains a challenger/source receipt only and should not ship as
  a runtime family.

This recommendation is not frozen until the controller accepts the rendered
checkpoint. No runtime font atlas, `TextRenderer`, `UiRenderer`, theme, icon,
or screen migration changed in Tasks 1-5.
